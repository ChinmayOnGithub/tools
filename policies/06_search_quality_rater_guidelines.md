# Google Search Quality Rater Guidelines

**Official PDF Document Link:** [https://guidelines.raterhub.com/searchqualityevaluatorguidelines.pdf](https://guidelines.raterhub.com/searchqualityevaluatorguidelines.pdf)

*(Note: The Search Quality Rater Guidelines document is over 170 pages long and frequently updated by Google to train human quality evaluators.)*

---

## Core Focus Areas for Evaluation

### 1. E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness)
- **Experience:** Does the content creator have first-hand or life experience with the topic?
- **Expertise:** Does the creator have the necessary knowledge or skill for the topic?
- **Authoritativeness:** Is the site or creator a recognized source on the topic?
- **Trustworthiness:** Is the page accurate, honest, safe, and reliable? (Trust is the central quality metric).

### 2. YMYL (Your Money or Your Life)
- Topics that could significantly impact a person's health, financial stability, safety, or well-being require the highest level of E-E-A-T.

### 3. Page Quality Rating Criteria
- **High Quality Content:** Needs clear main content, effort, original information, expertise, and a beneficial purpose.
- **Low Quality Content:** Lack of effort/originality, misleading info, intrusive ads, missing context or authorship details.
- **Lowest Quality Content:** Deceptive design, harmful or dangerous content, untrustworthy, automated/spun content without added value.

### 4. Needs Met Rating
- Measures how well search results satisfy the user's intent (Fully Meets, Highly Meets, Moderately Meets, Slightly Meets, Fails to Meet).
