### [](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/1378153?hl=en-GB&ref_topic=1250104&sjid=5169671761552421753-NC)I received a notification. What do I need to do next?

The notification contains details about the policy issue and an example URL where we found the issue. Some issues may also have associated [screenshots](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/13998398) in the Policy centre. Read the notification carefully and make the appropriate changes to your site to resolve the issue.

Sometimes an issue can be resolved by removing violating content or removing Google ads from those pages. In other cases, it may require changing the implementation of your ads. Bear in mind that the example URL is just one example. Review your entire site to ensure that the same issue doesn't exist on other pages.

Once you've fixed the issue, you can [request a review](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/7003627#request_review) of your site.

For subtitles in your language, turn on YouTube captions. Select the Settings icon ![Image 1: Image of YouTube settings icon](https://storage.googleapis.com/support-kms-prod/UrHzzlx74G5ig5vYXo9Nk43r7NM5fj72Tbu1) found in the video player, then select **Subtitles/CC** and choose your language.

### [](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/1378153?hl=en-GB&ref_topic=1250104&sjid=5169671761552421753-NC)What happens if I don't make changes?

If you do not make changes to bring your site into compliance, [ad serving will be disabled](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/113061) on your site.

If you missed the deadline and ad serving has already been disabled on your site, please make the appropriate changes and then [request a review](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/7003627#request_review) of your site.

### [](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/1378153?hl=en-GB&ref_topic=1250104&sjid=5169671761552421753-NC)I need more info about the policy that my site is violating. What should I do?

To learn more about a policy issue, visit your [Policy centre](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9485926), find the issue in the table and click **Fix** in the 'Action' column. This will take you to the 'Issue details' page where you’ll find a link to the policy, a description of the issue and instructions on how to resolve it.
